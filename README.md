# My Favorite Series

## FROM

**FROM** is my favorite television series. It is a *horror* and *mystery* show about people trapped in a strange town that they cannot escape.

### Why I Like It
- Exciting story
- Scary monsters
- Interesting characters
- Many mysteries

### Main Characters
1. Boyd Stevens
2. Tabitha Matthews
3. Jim Matthews

> FROM keeps me interested because every episode reveals new mysteries.

**Genre:** Horror, Mystery, Science Fiction
